- The repo is split into `frontend/` (Vite React SPA) and `backend/` (NestJS + Socket.IO), each with its own `package.json`. Run npm commands inside the relevant folder.
- Patients (list, profile, edit, treatment packages), Appointments (calendar + dashboard "Today’s schedule") and Settings → Treatments/Concerns use the API via TanStack Query (`src/components/patients/patients-api.ts`, `src/components/settings/catalog-settings.tsx`); the Billing page (`src/components/billing/billing-view.tsx`, `GET /api/billing/overview`) is live too; other screens still render mock data. `src/lib/api.ts` and `src/lib/socket.ts` are the integration points.
- Treatment plans (`backend/src/plans`, Settings → Treatment plans, `GET/POST/PATCH/DELETE /api/treatment-plans`, open to every role) are reusable combinations: steps (treatment, gap before it in days/weeks/months, reminder window ± days, complimentary, optional price/quantity) and repeat blocks (`{ repeat, steps }`). `plan-steps.ts` expands blocks and adds gaps. The frontend editor is `components/plans/plan-editor.tsx` (shared by Settings and the package builder).
- Backend data is in-memory (`backend/src/common/in-memory.repository.ts`) until a database is chosen.
- A package is a plan applied to a patient (`POST /api/patients/:id/packages` with `items`, `planId?`, `startDate`); each step is priced from Settings unless the doctor set a price (0 waives it) or marked it complimentary — the old fixed "FUE includes 3 free PRP" now lives in the seeded plan TP-2. Due dates aren't stored: `PackagesService.view` works them out — step 0 on the start date, each later step `gap` after the previous visit's actual procedure day (last day for multi-day surgery); until it happens, its booked day or due date stands in (`estimated`). Any step can be booked from the package (`POST /api/packages/:id/sessions/:index/appointment`; a missed visit is rescheduled in place); a normal booking of the same treatment takes the first open step of that treatment (`appointment.packageStep`). Pending bookings (`GET /api/packages/pending`) lists unbooked/missed surgery steps; `GET /api/packages/due?days=7` (not shown in the UI; kept for future patient reminders) lists each package's next non-surgery step once the previous one is done, from 7 days before its window. A package auto-completes when every step is done; cancelling removes its open visits. Surgery = treatments tagged `surgical` in Settings → Treatments; only surgery is scheduled from a package (Pending bookings / calendar day picker), other plan visits are booked as normal appointments. The UI's only reminder is Pending bookings (surgery). Surgery spans 1–3 days; only one surgery per day (`AppointmentsService.assertTheatreFree`); OPD is never blocked.
- Billing from the calendar: `GET /api/appointments/:id/billing` and `POST /api/appointments/:id/payments` (`backend/src/billing`). Every visit has its own invoice — package visits are billed per step (the step's quantity × the package's unit price; ₹0 / nothing to pay when complimentary or waived); other visits get their own invoice priced from Settings (or a charge entered with the first payment). Invoices track `paid`; statuses Pending → Partially paid → Paid. "Mark completed" opens a chargeable visit's bill as Pending (`BillingService.onAppointmentCompleted`; not for complimentary visits or ones with no price in Settings), so it shows on the Billing page and in the dashboard's collected-vs-billed bar (`revenue.unpaidVisits`); undoing completion drops that bill if nothing was paid. Each appointment carries `billStatus`, mirrored from its invoice via the internal `invoice.changed` event, and completed-but-unpaid visits get a "Payment pending" chip.
- EMI: `PUT/DELETE /api/appointments/:id/billing/emi` splits the current balance into installments (must sum to the balance, dates ascending, first not in the past). Payments made after the plan starts fill installments in due-date order (`installmentViews` in `billing.service.ts`).
- Billing actions exist per appointment (`/api/appointments/:id/...`) and per invoice (`/api/invoices/:id/billing|payments|billing/emi`); the frontend addresses a bill by `BillPath`. Payments carry `receivedAt`; seeded paid invoices have matching seeded payments.
- Appointment status is never edited directly: booking → Scheduled; moving date/time of a Scheduled visit → Rescheduled; `POST/DELETE /api/appointments/:id/check-in` (tick in day lists, only on/after the day; sets the patient's lastVisit) → Checked in; `POST /api/appointments/:id/complete` ("Mark completed", only after check-in) → Completed. Checked-in/completed visits can't be rescheduled. Visits still Scheduled/Rescheduled after their day become Missed (`AppointmentsService.sweepMissed`, on every read and every 10 min); Missed visits stay editable — reschedule (→ Rescheduled) or check in.
- Dashboard cards (`GET /api/dashboard/summary`): Total patients, Revenue this month, PRP sessions, Avg grafts this month (subtitle: this month's completed surgeries and total grafts). Grafts use only `PackageStep.actualGrafts`, the count entered after surgery via `PATCH /api/packages/:id/sessions/:index/grafts` — never the estimate given when the package was created; completed surgeries without it are reported as `awaitingCount`.
- Completed appointments are read-only (PATCH → 400); the details dialog shows them as "Appointment details" with every field disabled, but Receive payment still works.
- Inventory (`backend/src/inventory`, sidebar → Inventory, `components/inventory/`): products the clinic stocks (medicines, shampoos, oils…). The SKU/barcode is the record's `id` — sent as `itemId` on `POST /api/inventory`, unique case-insensitively (409), URL-safe characters only, never editable. Prices are INR with up to 2 decimals. `POST /api/inventory/:id/stock { change }` restocks (+) or records a sale (−) and can't go below 0; any role can add/edit/adjust, only Admin deletes. Dropping to the reorder level pushes a "Low stock"/"Out of stock" bell notification. Low stock (≤ reorder level) and expiry (expired / within 90 days) are worked out in the frontend (`inventory-api.ts`). 12 sample products (every type, each with an SVG image in `frontend/public/products/`) are always seeded; `imageUrl` accepts an http(s) URL or a site path like `/products/x.svg`.
